# Gatekeeper — 3-minute demo script

**Before going on stage:** open http://localhost:5173 in a ~1440px window as the lead of **Grok Night London**, click **Reset demo** (top right) and confirm. You should see 6 waiting cards, "model retrained on 120 decisions", and 9 Shipped builders seats left. Keep a second tab open on the public form (`/apply/grok-night-london`).

| Time | Show | Say |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:10 | Review queue | "Community leads vet hundreds of applications by hand, scammers move between communities, and people declined once are never looked at again. Gatekeeper fixes all three, with a reason for every call." |
| 0:10–0:45 | Public form tab. Fill in **Ada**, `ada@example.com`, GitHub `demo-ada-ships`, Devpost `demo-ada-ships`, category *Shipped builders*, and paste: *I shipped Civic Lens, an AI tool that summarises London council meetings; the repo is public, I demo it at meetups and help others debug their LLM apps.* Submit. | "A strong builder applies. We check her against the lead's written rules plus GitHub and Devpost evidence, and a model trained on this lead's past decisions adjusts the call. Accepted, with the reasons: account age, repos, Devpost wins, rule by rule." Switch to **Applicants**: "Shipped builders went from 9 seats left to 8. Once a category is full, people are waitlisted, ranked by score." |
| 0:45–1:25 | **Review queue**. Press `2` (evidence) and `3` (history model) on Yasmin, then `A`. Press `D` on the next card and pick a reason. | "Clear cases are decided automatically. Borderline ones come here, one at a time, for example when the rules and the lead's history disagree. Keyboard only: accept, decline with a reason. Each click is training data." Point at the badge: "model retrained on 121… 122 decisions." |
| 1:25–2:00 | Public form: **Dave**, `dave@example.com`, phone `07700 900123`, GitHub `demo-crypto-dave`, *Shipped builders*, text *I build crypto communities, DM me for guaranteed returns.* Submit. | "Dave was reported and confirmed by another community, Shoreditch Builders Club. Communities share only HMAC hashes of normalised phone numbers and handles, never the raw numbers. Declined, with the evidence. Row-level security in Postgres means unconfirmed reports never leave the community that filed them." (Optional: Blocklist page.) |
| 2:00–2:30 | **Applicants** → *Maya Learns* (declined 75 days ago, skill gap) → **Re-check now**. | "A skill gap is temporary. After a 60-day cool-down, Gatekeeper re-checks the evidence, daily or on demand. Maya now has 14 repos and a Devpost win, so she's accepted, and the note says why. Scam declines are never reconsidered." |
| 2:30–3:00 | **Evaluation** | "We measured it on 150 held-out synthetic applicants: rules only versus rules plus the history model. Real people wrongly declined drop from 28% to 5%, and on cases it decides itself it's right 93% of the time. The trade-off is more cases go to the lead (15% to 23%), mostly first-timers and researchers the written rules undervalue. The data is synthetic and we say so on the page." |

**If something goes wrong:** Gemini unavailable → point at the badge: "the LLM is down, so it falls back to deterministic rule checks, labelled; it never fakes model output." Applying twice with the same handles is fine. Run **Reset demo** between rehearsals.
